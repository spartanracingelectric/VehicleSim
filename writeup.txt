recent commits (the last one is the big one, "independent tire physics, regen fix, brakes flags, and filter"):

we went from a two axle model to a four wheel model. every wheel now has its own load, slip ratio, slip angle, wheel speed, motor torque, brake torque and tire force. the car is one body with forward speed, sideways speed and yaw rate, pushed by the sum of the four tires. weight now moves front to back under accel and braking, and side to side in corners (lateral accel times cg height over track, split front and rear by a roll stiffness fraction). that is what torque vectoring needs, since it only means something if the left and right wheels can see different loads and slip. each wheel is also stepped implicitly (backward euler on the linearized tire force). at low speed the slip ratio changes way faster than the 100 us step, so a plain step would overshoot and ring. the implicit step always settles, and it still lets the wheel lock or spin past the peak of the curve like a real tire would.

on the tire side there is now one Tire class holding one set of numbers, and you pick dugoff or pacejka with a flag. every wheel gets its own copy of the model because the models remember their last slip and forces. brakes got per wheel regen so the motors can give different regen on each wheel, and the friction brakes only make up what is left of the pedal request. we also added optional first order filters on slip and tire force, off by default, just in case the forces buzz.

what is missing is mostly real numbers. track width and roll stiffness split are placeholders (1.2 m and 50/50) until we have SR18 values. the pacejka C and E values are just usual starting points and still need a fit to the TTC data, and nelson's mu of 1.2 vs tejas's 1.5 is not settled. the load transfer is simple, no roll centers, and a wheel that lifts off can leave the load sum a bit short. also there is no load sensitivity on mu, no hydraulic lag or pad temperature on the brakes, and no transient tire response beyond the optional filters.

one thing i should flag, i didn't run any of the tests, this is just from reading the diffs.
